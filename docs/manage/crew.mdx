**Crew** are your organization's frontline workers — the people who use a
[custom app](/guide/build/sdk) on a shared tablet in a store, not at a desk.
They don't need an email address or an Oxygen account: a worker signs in with
their identifier and a PIN, and only on a tablet you've enrolled as a
**kiosk**.

Crew are managed by organization Owners and Admins from
**Settings → Organization → Crew**.

## Enroll a worker

1. Open **Settings → Organization → Crew** and select **Enroll worker**.
2. Enter their **Name**, an **Identifier** (an employee number or short
   handle, unique within the organization), and a 4–8 digit **PIN**.
3. Choose the **Apps** they can open.

From a worker's row you can change their apps, record **where they work**
(location, position, and who they report to), **Reset PIN…**, or **Suspend**
them — a suspended worker can't sign in until you **Reinstate** them.

## Enroll a kiosk

A PIN only works on an enrolled kiosk, so a worker's PIN is useless on any
other device.

1. On the **Kiosks** tab, select **New kiosk**.
2. Name it (for example, *Front counter*), and optionally choose the
   **Location** it sits at and the app it **Opens** to.
3. Set how long it waits before it **signs itself out** — 30 minutes by
   default.
4. Open the **Enroll link** on the tablet — copy it, or scan its QR code.

If a link is lost, create a new one from the kiosk's row. **Revoke kiosk**
stops the tablet from accepting sign-ins.

## Signing in on a kiosk

An enrolled kiosk's login page shows **Who's on shift?** instead of the normal
sign-in. The worker enters their ID and PIN, and lands in the kiosk's app.
Admins can still reach the normal sign-in from the kiosk with **Sign in as an
admin**.

When a custom app signs a worker out, the kiosk returns to **Who's on shift?**
for the next person.

## What crew can and can't do

- Crew only see the apps you grant them. They are not organization members and
  don't appear in **Settings → Organization → Members**.
- Inside an app's functions, a crew member is identified by `ctx.user` with
  `email: null`, and their **reach** — the locations they're assigned to —
  decides what data the app shows them. See
  [Oxygen Functions](/guide/build/sdk/functions).
