Everyone in your organization holds one of three roles:

- **Owner** — full control of the organization, including billing.
- **Admin** — can invite members, change settings, and edit any project.
- **Member** — can build agents, run Automations, and ask questions.

Manage members from **Settings → Organization → Members**. Bulk invites are
supported.

Two rules protect the organization: only an **Owner** can change another Owner or
Admin, and an organization always keeps at least one Owner (transfer ownership
before removing the last one).

## Workspace roles

A member's organization role carries into every workspace. To give someone a
different role in one workspace, change it in **Settings → Workspace →
Members**. Workspace roles are **Owner**, **Admin**, **Member**, and
**Viewer** — a Viewer can read but not change the workspace.

## Teams and app access

Admins can group members into **Teams** (**Settings → Organization → Teams**)
and decide which [custom apps](/guide/build/sdk) each team can open from
**Settings → Organization → App access**.

Frontline workers who sign in on shared store tablets are managed separately —
see [Crew & kiosks](/guide/manage/crew).

For the full picture — including **partner roles**, where one organization manages
another — see [Permissions](/guide/manage/permissions).
