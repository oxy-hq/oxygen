Google OAuth lets your team sign in to Oxygen with their existing
Google account. There's no password to manage — the Google sign-in
flow handles identity and MFA.

## Set it up

1. Go to **Manage → Authentication → Google** in your workspace.
2. Toggle **Enable Google sign-in**.
3. (Optional) Restrict sign-ins to specific Google Workspace domains —
   useful for keeping access scoped to `@acme.com` employees only.

After enabling, the workspace sign-in screen shows a **Sign in with
Google** button. New users land in your workspace with the default
role you configured in **Settings → Organization → Members**.

## Coexisting with magic link

Google OAuth can run alongside [magic link](/guide/manage/authentication/magic-link).
Users see both options and pick one.
