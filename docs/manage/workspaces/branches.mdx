title: Branches and editing
description: How a workspace's files change — in its repository, with a push

---

A workspace's agents, automations, semantic model and `config.yml` are files
in its Git repository, and that repository is where they change. Oxygen no
longer edits files in the product: the in-app editor, its branch switcher and
its commit and push buttons were removed.

To change a workspace:

1. Open the Developer Portal and choose **Work in your editor** in the
   sidebar. It shows the repository, a `git clone` line, an **Open in VS
   Code** link and the `oxyc workspace clone` command.
2. Edit the files with your own editor or an agent, on any branch you like.
3. Push to the workspace's default branch. The push compiles and deploys the
   change; nobody needs to press anything in Oxygen.

To try a branch before it reaches the default branch, push it and open a
preview of it (**Settings → Previews**).

A workspace with no repository has nowhere to edit: connect one in
**Settings** first.
