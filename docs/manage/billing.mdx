Oxygen Cloud bills per seat through Stripe. Admins can:

- View invoices and payment methods in the billing portal
- Add or remove seats
- Upgrade or downgrade plans

From **Settings → Organization → Billing**, click **Open billing portal** to launch the
self-serve Stripe portal.

[Enterprise customers](/guide/manage/enterprise) are billed by annual
contract; the portal still surfaces invoices.
