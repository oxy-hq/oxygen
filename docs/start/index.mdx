Oxygen Nexus takes a novel view on enterprise software: **your business is a
physics problem.** The work is to model your business as a set of physical
equations, and then forecast, backtest, and change business output.

This approach requires a **formula-first, physics-first World Model** — one
that becomes a Digital Twin of the business. In the World Model, every KPI is
defined as an equation over its drivers. Oxygen Nexus builds that model of
your operations, then deploys bespoke Applications and Agents on top of it
that use AI and software to make more revenue and reduce costs.

Oxygen Nexus is delivered as a hosted product at **[app.oxygen-hq.com](https://app.oxygen-hq.com)**.
Sign up, connect your data, and start asking questions.
