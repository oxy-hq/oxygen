# Evidence coverage (california_schools)

One line per evidence string (verbatim) → the member(s) that carry it.

- 'Free or reduced-price meal recipients ages 5-17' refers to the count reported in the meal program report for students ages 5-17 receiving free or reduced-price meals. → `frpm.frpm_count_ages_5_17`, `frpm.total_frpm_count_ages_5_17`
- 'P' stands for preschool. → `frpm.low_grade` / `frpm.high_grade` (value 'P' = preschool), `schools.grade_span_offered`; `schools.virtual` notes 'P' there means partial virtual, not preschool
- A Youth Authority school is a school the state directory classifies as a Youth Authority Facility. → `schools.soc_type` = 'Youth Authority Facilities' (soc '11'; alternative `schools.edops_code` 'YTH' named in the description)
- A school counts as exclusive-virtual only if its virtual-instruction category is coded 'F'. → `schools.virtual` (= 'F'), `schools.virtual_label`, `schools.exclusively_virtual_school_count`
- A school district is a local education agency that runs K-12 public schools; it is an elementary school district, a high school district, or a unified school district. → `schools.is_school_district`, `schools.doc` ('52','54','56'), `schools.doc_type`, `frpm.district_type`
- A school's total SAT score is the sum of its average reading, average math, and average writing scores; a school's mailing city is the city in its mailing address. → `satscores.total_sat_score`, `satscores.avg_total_sat_score`, `schools.mail_city`
- A state special school is a school operated by the state of California that serves students with special needs, such as the schools for the deaf or the blind. → `schools.edops_code` ('SSS'), `schools.soc` / `schools.soc_type` ('31' / 'State Special Schools'), `schools.doc` ('31'), `frpm.school_type`, `frpm.educational_option_type`
- An exclusively virtual school is one whose Virtual field holds the code 'F'. → `schools.virtual` (= 'F'), `schools.exclusively_virtual_school_count`
- Breakfast Provision 2: the NSLP provision status category the school participates in. CDS code 1612590136051: the school identifier being asked about. → `frpm.nslp_provision_status` (exact value 'Breakfast Provision 2'), `schools.cds_code` / `frpm.cds_code` (13-digit code = stored value minus leading zero, '01612590136051')
- CDS code is the California Department of Education's school identifier; a charter school is one with charter status recorded as 1. → `schools.cds_code`, `schools.charter` (= 1), `schools.charter_school_count`, `frpm.charter_school_yn`
- CDS code: a school's identifier in the California schools data. → `schools.cds_code`, `frpm.cds_code`, `satscores.cds`
- CDS code: the California Department of Education's identification code for a school; the benchmark school in my notes is identified by CDS code 4615150435602. → `schools.cds_code` (13-digit form -> '04615150435602'), `frpm.cds_code`, `satscores.cds`
- CDS code: the County-District-School identifier assigned to each school; scoring at least 1500: a test taker whose combined SAT score is 1500 or higher. → `schools.cds_code`, `satscores.num_ge1500` (scoring at least 1500 = total SAT >= 1500)
- CDS code: the County-District-School identifier that the California Department of Education assigns to each school and district in the state. → `schools.cds_code` (district rows carry school code '0000000'), `schools` view description
- CDS code: the identification number the California Department of Education assigns to a county, district, or school. Directly-funded charter school: a charter school that receives its funding directly from the state rather than through its district. → `schools.cds_code`, `schools.funding_type` ('Directly funded'), `frpm.charter_funding_type`
- CDS code: the state's County-District-School identification code assigned to each school; FRPM: the Free or Reduced Price Meal program, where the FRPM count is the number of students eligible. → `schools.cds_code`, `frpm.frpm_count_k12`, `frpm.frpm_count_ages_5_17`, `frpm` view description
- Charter number 756 is the state-issued charter number. → `schools.charter_number` (stored '0756'), `frpm.charter_school_number`
- Directly funded and locally funded are the two charter funding categories. Free or reduced-price meal eligibility is measured by the K-12 eligible percentage. Combined SAT score is the sum of the average reading, math and writing scores. → `schools.funding_type`, `frpm.charter_funding_type`, `frpm.eligible_frpm_pct_k12` / `frpm.eligible_frpm_rate_k12`, `frpm.frpm_rate_k12`, `satscores.total_sat_score`
- FRPM eligibility percentage is free-or-reduced-price meal recipients divided by same-age-band enrollment, times 100. → `frpm.eligible_frpm_pct_k12`, `frpm.eligible_frpm_pct_ages_5_17`
- FRPM means free or reduced price meal eligibility. → `frpm.frpm_count_k12`, `frpm.eligible_frpm_rate_k12`, `frpm` view description
- Free meal percentage = free meal count (K-12) / enrollment (K-12) * 100; FRPM percentage = FRPM count (K-12) / enrollment (K-12) * 100; charter school = charter indicator 1, non-charter = charter indicator 0. → `frpm.eligible_free_pct_k12`, `frpm.eligible_frpm_pct_k12`, `schools.charter` / `frpm.charter_school_yn` (1 / 0)
- Free meal rate = Free Meal Count (K-12) / Enrollment (K-12). SAT performance level is based on the SAT total (Reading + Math + Writing): Below Average if below 1200, Average if 1200 to 1500, Above Average if above 1500, and 'No SAT Data' if no SAT scores are available. → `frpm.eligible_free_rate_k12`, `frpm.free_meal_rate_k12`, `satscores.sat_performance_level`, `satscores.total_sat_score`
- In the school directory, the exclusive-virtual category is coded 'F' and the partial-virtual category is coded 'P'. → `schools.virtual` ('F' / 'P'), `schools.virtual_label`
- K-12 enrollment is enrollment counted for kindergarten through grade 12. Enrollment of ages 5-17 is enrollment counted for students aged 5 through 17. Highest grade of enrollment is the highest grade level the school enrolls students in (e.g., grade 8 for a school serving kindergarten through grade 8). → `frpm.enrollment_k12`, `frpm.enrollment_ages_5_17`, `frpm.high_grade`, `frpm.high_grade_number`

- NSLP is the National School Lunch Program. Provision 2 is a federal provision under the NSLP that lets a school serve meals at no charge to all students regardless of family income. → `frpm.nslp_provision_status` (description)
- Provision 2 school: a school whose NSLP Provision Status is Provision 2. Magnet school: a school identified as a magnet school or providing a magnet program. → `frpm.nslp_provision_status` (= 'Provision 2'), `schools.magnet` (= 1), `schools.magnet_school_count`

- SAT test takers: the number of test takers recorded for the school in the SAT scores data. → `satscores.num_test_takers`, `satscores.total_test_takers`
- SAT test takers: the number of test takers recorded for the school. → `satscores.num_test_takers`, `satscores.total_test_takers`
