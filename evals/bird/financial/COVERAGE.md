# Evidence coverage (financial)

One line per evidence string (verbatim) → the member(s) that carry it.

- 'End of 1995' means the state on 31 December 1995, and the AB transaction may date from any time, before or after that date; 'AB' is the code identifying the partner bank of the transaction. → `trans.balance` (description: state at a date = balance after last transaction on/before it), `trans.partner_bank` (sample 'AB', description), `trans` view description
- 'UROK' is the bank's code for interest credited to an account; penalty (sanction) interest charged to an account uses the bank's sanction-interest code, which is built on the same interest code. → `trans.k_symbol` (description + samples 'UROK', 'SANKC. UROK'), `trans.total_interest_credited`, `trans.total_sanction_interest`
- A gold card is a card of type 'gold', and its holder is the client linked to its account as type 'OWNER'. The district of an account is the district of the branch that holds it; a district's inhabitants, salary and urban-inhabitant ratio are the district's own figures. → `card.card_type`, `card.gold_card_count`, `card` view description, `disp.disposition_type`, `account.district_id`, `district` view description, `district.inhabitants`, `district.average_salary`, `district.urban_inhabitant_ratio`
- A loan is repaid over a term that starts on its approval day and lasts the number of months recorded for the loan; the loan is being repaid on every day of its term. Status 'C' means a running contract, OK so far; status 'D' means a running contract, client in debt. The regions are the bank's district regions. → `loan.approved_date`, `loan.duration`, `loan.end_date`, `loan.status`, `loan.is_running`, `district.region` / `client_district.region`
- A transaction with no characterization recorded means the bank did not tag it with any characterization. → `trans.k_symbol` (NULL or ' '), `trans.has_characterization`, `orders.k_symbol` ('')
- An account owner is the client recorded as 'OWNER' on the account. An outgoing payment is a transaction recorded with type 'VYDAJ' (money paid out of the account). An external account is an account held at another bank to which the account holder sends money; the receiving bank together with the receiving account number identifies it. → `disp.disposition_type`, `disp.owner_count`, `trans.transaction_type`, `trans.total_expense`, `orders.bank_to`, `orders.account_to`, `orders` view description
- An external counterparty account is an account at another bank that money was transferred to or received from. Amount received refers to credit transactions, which carry transaction type PRIJEM. Amount withdrawn refers to debit transactions, which carry transaction type VYDAJ. → `trans.partner_bank`, `trans.partner_account`, `trans.total_income`, `trans.total_expense`, `trans.transaction_type`
- An outside bank counterparty is a bank account at another institution that money was sent to or received from on a transfer transaction. District population is the number of inhabitants of the district. → `trans.partner_bank`, `trans.partner_account`, `trans.operation`, `trans` view description, `district.inhabitants` / `client_district.inhabitants`
- Average salary means the average yearly salary recorded for a district, in CZK. → `district.average_salary` / `client_district.average_salary`
- Average salary refers to the district attribute describing average salary. → `district.average_salary` / `client_district.average_salary`
- Balance means the balance after a transaction. → `trans.balance`
- By "home district" I mean the district a client lives in, and a district's inhabitants are its number of inhabitants. → `client.district_id`, `client` entity `home_district`, `client_district` view, `client_district.inhabitants`
- Central Bohemia is the region of the Czech Republic surrounding Prague. → `district.region` / `client_district.region` (value 'central Bohemia')
- Central Bohemia is the region value recorded for districts in the region data; the amount lent on an account is the total of the loans granted on that account. → `district.region` / `client_district.region`, `loan.total_loan_amount`, `loan` view description
- Disposition type OWNER means the client owns the account. Unemployment rate in 1996 is the district's unemployment rate for 1996; average salary is the district's average salary. → `disp.disposition_type`, `district.unemployment_rate_1996`, `district.average_salary`
- East Bohemia refers to the districts in the East Bohemia region of the Czech Republic. → `district.region` / `client_district.region` (value 'east Bohemia')
- Female means client gender = 'F'. Deposits are transactions recorded as PRIJEM (credits to the account). District average salary is the district's average salary figure. → `client.gender`, `client.female_client_count`, `trans.transaction_type`, `trans.total_income`, `district.average_salary`
- Gold credit card means card type 'gold'; account owner means disposition type 'OWNER'; the counter-account is the partner account at another bank to which a payment is sent; the district is the one where the account's branch is located. → `card.card_type`, `card.gold_card_count`, `disp.disposition_type`, `orders.account_to`, `orders.bank_to`, `trans.partner_account`, `account.district_id`, `district` view description
- Loan repayment status codes: A means the contract finished and was paid off, B means the loan was not repaid (default), C means the loan is running and being repaid, D means the loan is running and the client is in debt on it. Transaction types: a credit transaction brings money into the account, a debit transaction takes money out, and a cash withdrawal takes money out in cash. → `loan.status`, `loan.status_label`, `loan.is_running`, `trans.transaction_type` (PRIJEM / VYDAJ / VYBER), `trans.transaction_type_label`, `trans.total_cash_withdrawal_vyber`
- Loan status 'D' denotes a running contract with the client in debt. → `loan.status`, `loan.in_debt_loan_count`
- Loan status C means an active contract in good standing; loan status D means an active contract with the client in debt. Both are active contracts. → `loan.status`, `loan.is_running`, `loan.running_loan_count`, `loan.ok_running_loan_count`, `loan.in_debt_loan_count`
- Loan status C means the contract is running with no problems so far. District average salary is the average salary recorded for a district. → `loan.status`, `loan.ok_running_loan_count`, `district.average_salary`
- Monthly statement issuance means the account's statement frequency is 'POPLATEK MESICNE'. → `account.frequency`, `account.frequency_label`
- OWNER is the disposition type marking an account's owner; DISPONENT marks other account users. → `disp.disposition_type`, `disp.owner_count`, `disp.disponent_count`
- PRIJEM = incoming transaction; VYDAJ = outgoing transaction; net balance = total income - total expense → `trans.transaction_type`, `trans.total_income`, `trans.total_expense`, `trans.net_balance`
- Payment purpose: the stated reason or note recorded for a transaction. → `trans.k_symbol`, `orders.k_symbol`
- SIPO stands for household payment. → `trans.k_symbol`, `trans.total_household_payments`, `orders.k_symbol`
- The bank's first month of operation is the calendar month in which its earliest account was opened. → `account.first_account_opened_date`, `account.opened_month`, `account` view description
- The owner of an account is the client with the OWNER disposition; a DISPONENT is a co-holder who does not own it. A loan is a credit granted on the account, recorded with the date it was approved. → `disp.disposition_type`, `loan` view description, `loan.approved_date`
- VYDAJ denotes an outgoing payment (debit); PRIJEM denotes an incoming payment (credit). → `trans.transaction_type`, `trans.total_expense`, `trans.total_income`
- VYDAJ marks a transaction where money leaves the account. SIPO is the code for household bill payments. A payment states its purpose through its transaction characterization. → `trans.transaction_type`, `trans.k_symbol`, `trans.total_household_payments`, `orders.k_symbol`
- Weekly statement issuance means account issuance frequency 'POPLATEK TYDNE'. A district's inhabitants are its number of inhabitants, and its average salary is its average yearly salary. → `account.frequency`, `account.frequency_label`, `district.inhabitants`, `district.average_salary`
- Weekly statement issuance means statement frequency is 'POPLATEK TYDNE'; loan duration is stated in months. → `account.frequency`, `loan.duration`, `loan.end_date`
- Withdrawal transactions are those recorded with type = 'VYDAJ'; a standing order is a recurring payment order that debits the account. → `trans.transaction_type`, `trans.total_expense`, `orders` view description, `orders.amount`
